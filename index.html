    About this atlas

    A real scan, read like a textbook plate

    Anatomy Atlas loads a scanned 3D muscular figure and scatters it with clickable pins, colour-coded by body system. The model turns on its own so you can watch it from every angle; drag any time to take over.

    Every pin carries its own entry — official name, common synonyms, location, size, function and clinical relevance — covering the circulatory, respiratory, nervous, digestive, musculoskeletal and immune systems.

    This is a study aid, produced for an educational brief. It is not a diagnostic or clinical-grade reference.
